Currently working on

Oculus-controlled robotic arm

I modelled the entire arm myself in SolidWorks and 3D printed every part from scratch — base, links, joints and gripper, no kit involved. It runs on servos off an Arduino, and I'm teleoperating it with Oculus Touch controllers: move your hand, the arm follows.

  • Every component designed from the ground up in SolidWorks, then printed and assembled at home
  • Prototyped all the control code myself to get it to where it is now — servo drivers, joint limits, and inverse kinematics running on the microcontroller
  • Mapping Oculus controller pose and trigger input to joint angles in real time
  • Next step: recording teleop sessions as demonstrations and training the arm to repeat tasks on its own
